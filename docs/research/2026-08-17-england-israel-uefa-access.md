# 2026/27 UEFA access: England and Israel

Checked: 17 August 2026  
Scope: men's UEFA club competitions; ordinary domestic access, cup cascades, EPS and titleholder exceptions  
Source policy: UEFA, Premier League and Israel Football Association sources only

## Executive answer

- **England has two ordinary Europa League berths, never three:** one league route and the FA Cup route. Its ordinary seven-club allocation is four UCL, two UEL and one UECL. An EPS, or a UEFA titleholder qualifying outside the relevant domestic places, can add a club without becoming a third ordinary domestic UEL berth.
- **Israel has four ordinary berths:** champion to UCL Q2 Champions Path; State Cup winner to UEL Q2 Main Path; two league qualifiers to UECL Q2 Main Path.
- A domestic cup runner-up does **not** inherit a UEFA berth. When the cup winner has already qualified at the same or a higher level, the applicable place is reassigned through the league under UEFA Articles 3.03–3.05.
- Resolve titleholders and EPS as separate entitlements before the final domestic cascade. They have special non-pass-down cases and cannot safely be modelled as extra league positions.

## England

### Ordinary domestic allocation

| Sporting route | 2026/27 entry | Ordinary places |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Premier League positions 1–4 | Champions League league phase | 4 |
| Highest league finisher below the UCL qualifiers (normally fifth) | Europa League league phase | 1 |
| FA Cup winner | Europa League league phase | 1 |
| EFL Cup winner | Conference League play-off | 1 |

This is the Premier League's published baseline and UEFA's rule for an association with two UEL representatives: the domestic cup winner plus the best league finisher below its UCL qualifiers. Sources: [Premier League qualification explainer](https://www.premierleague.com/en/european-qualification-explained) and [UEFA Europa League regulations, Article 3.02](https://documents.uefa.com/r/Regulations-of-the-UEFA-Europa-League-2026/27/Article-3-Entries-for-the-competition-Online).

The EFL Cup is England's UEFA-approved special competition for its lowest Conference League route. It does not create another Europa League route. See [UEFA Conference League regulations, Articles 3.02–3.06](https://documents.uefa.com/r/Regulations-of-the-UEFA-Conference-League-2026/27/Article-3-Entries-for-the-competition-Online).

### Ordinary cup reallocations

1. If the **FA Cup winner** has already qualified for the UCL or UEL through its league position, the FA Cup's UEL place goes to the highest Premier League finisher not yet qualified for UEFA competition. England still has two ordinary UEL places; both may simply appear as league-position qualifiers.
2. If the **EFL Cup winner** has already qualified for the UCL or UEL through domestic league/cup performance, the UECL play-off place goes to the highest unqualified Premier League finisher.
3. A UEFA-titleholder overlap is not always the same as an ordinary domestic overlap. The Premier League explicitly notes that when the EFL Cup winner reaches UCL/UEL **solely by winning a UEFA competition while outside the domestic European positions**, the UECL place is forfeited rather than passed down. Sources: [Premier League FA Cup/EFL Cup route table](https://www.premierleague.com/en/european-qualification-explained) and [Premier League EFL Cup reallocation explainer](https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/4580801/how-man-citys-efl-cup-win-affects-qualification-for-uefa-competitions).

### European Performance Spot

An EPS is an **additional UCL league-phase place** for each of the two best-performing associations in the immediately preceding UEFA season. It goes to that association's highest domestic finisher not already in the UCL league phase. For England this is normally fifth; the ordinary league UEL route then moves to sixth, while the FA Cup and EFL Cup routes remain. Sources: [UEFA 2026/27 UCL access breakdown](https://www.uefa.com/uefachampionsleague/news/02a2-1fdbe9a25733-8d37ff5f9226-1000--2026-27-uefa-champions-league-which-teams-are-in-the-euro/) and [UEFA UCL regulations, Articles 3.08–3.09](https://documents.uefa.com/r/Regulations-of-the-UEFA-Champions-League-2026/27/Article-3-Entries-for-the-competition-Online).

Rare overlap: if fifth has already entered the UCL as UCL/UEL titleholder, the EPS goes to sixth, but the UEL entitlement occupied by that titleholder is not also passed to another English club. Do not produce both an EPS club and a replacement for a lower berth that UEFA requires the association to surrender.

England earned an EPS for 2026/27. The Premier League's final access table shows how the routes combine: five UCL clubs, two ordinary UEL qualifiers after domestic cascades, an additional UEL club as UECL holder, and one UECL qualifier. [Premier League 2026/27 route table](https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/4671711/how-crystal-palace-uefa-conference-league-final-affects-european-qualification).

### UEFA titleholders

- The **UCL holder** and **UEL holder** are guaranteed the following UCL league phase.
- If either holder already has an English UCL place, England receives no replacement domestic UCL berth; UEFA rebalances the vacancy elsewhere in the access list.
- If a UCL/UEL holder finishes outside all English European positions, its UCL place is additional and England's ordinary qualifiers remain.
- If that holder occupied an English UEL or UECL domestic place, England's entitlement in that lower competition is reduced by one; it is not passed down domestically.
- The **UECL holder** is guaranteed the following UEL league phase. It is additional if the club did not otherwise qualify for the UEL. If it already holds an ordinary UEL berth, it does not create a third English entrant; if it occupied the domestic UECL berth, that UECL berth is lost. If it qualifies domestically for the UCL, it plays in the UCL and the UEL titleholder vacancy is rebalanced by UEFA rather than passed down in England.

These are entitlement and vacancy rules, not league-position cascades. Sources: [UEFA UCL regulations, Articles 3.01–3.07](https://documents.uefa.com/r/Regulations-of-the-UEFA-Champions-League-2026/27/Article-3-Entries-for-the-competition-Online) and [UEFA UEL regulations, Articles 3.07–3.13](https://documents.uefa.com/r/Regulations-of-the-UEFA-Europa-League-2026/27/Article-3-Entries-for-the-competition-Online).

### Can England have three Europa League clubs?

**Yes in the field, no in ordinary domestic allocation.** England's ordinary domestic allocation always supplies two UEL berths. A third English UEL entrant is possible only through an independent UEFA entitlement, most notably a UECL holder that did not already occupy one of those two UEL berths. That is exactly why England has three UEL entrants in 2026/27: the third is the UECL titleholder, not another league/FA Cup place. [Premier League 2026/27 route table](https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/4671711/how-crystal-palace-uefa-conference-league-final-affects-european-qualification).

## Israel

### Ordinary domestic allocation

| Sporting route | 2026/27 entry | Ordinary places |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Israeli Premier League champion | Champions League second qualifying round, Champions Path | 1 |
| State Cup winner | Europa League second qualifying round, Main Path | 1 |
| Highest two league finishers not allocated a higher route | Conference League second qualifying round, Main Path | 2 |

The actual 2026/27 draws validate all four routes: Hapoel Beer-Sheva entered [UCL Q2 Champions Path](https://www.uefa.com/uefachampionsleague/news/02a6-20df98635dbd-bd0f9ed2d71e-1000--uefa-champions-league-second-qualifying-round-draw/), Maccabi Tel-Aviv entered [UEL Q2 Main Path](https://www.uefa.com/uefaeuropaleague/news/02a6-20df986c481c-c6f50cdfcae5-1000--uefa-europa-league-second-qualifying-round-draw/), and Beitar Jerusalem plus Hapoel Tel-Aviv entered [UECL Q2 Main Path](https://www.uefa.com/uefaconferenceleague/news/02a6-20df98748570-c0cad182ec88-1000--uefa-conference-league-second-qualifying-round-draw/).

The IFA's final table records Hapoel Beer-Sheva first, Beitar Jerusalem second, Maccabi Tel-Aviv third and Hapoel Tel-Aviv fourth; the IFA records Maccabi Tel-Aviv as State Cup winner. This demonstrates the cup priority in practice: third-place Maccabi took the UEL cup route, while the two UECL routes went to second and fourth. Sources: [IFA 2025/26 league table](https://www.football.org.il/en/team-details/?season_id=27&team_id=1061) and [IFA 2025/26 State Cup](https://www.football.org.il/national-cup/?national_cup_id=618&season_id=27).

### State Cup double cases

- **Cup winner is champion/UCL qualifier:** retain the club's UCL route; give the UEL cup route to the highest eligible league finisher, then give the two UECL routes to the next two eligible league finishers.
- **Cup winner finishes in a normal UECL league position:** give it the higher-priority UEL cup route; pass the two UECL league routes to the next eligible finishers. The 2026/27 Maccabi example is this case.
- **Cup runner-up:** receives nothing merely for being runner-up.

These cascades preserve Israel's fixed `1 UCL + 1 UEL + 2 UECL` ordinary quota while respecting the cup winner's priority. [UEFA UEL regulations, Articles 3.02–3.05](https://documents.uefa.com/r/Regulations-of-the-UEFA-Europa-League-2026/27/Article-3-Entries-for-the-competition-Online) and [UEFA UECL regulations, Articles 3.02–3.06](https://documents.uefa.com/r/Regulations-of-the-UEFA-Conference-League-2026/27/Article-3-Entries-for-the-competition-Online).

## Goalbound audit: implementation constraints

The active resolver is `features/career/uefaSeason.ts`; `features/career/world.ts` imports it. The audit found these mismatches relevant to England and Israel:

1. England's FA Cup and EFL Cup winners are added with a global `taken` check, but a failed add is not replaced by an explicit highest-unqualified English club. A winner already qualified higher can therefore make the cup place disappear instead of cascade.
2. Israel's champion is correctly selected into UCL Q2 Champions Path by association rank, but its State Cup UEL Q2 route and its two UECL Q2 routes are not represented as explicit association entitlements. Generic `addUntil` filling cannot guarantee the correct clubs, count or round.
3. UCL/UEL titleholders are added before domestic selection, but the later `nextAvailable` logic can pass their vacated lower English place down when UEFA requires England's lower-comp entitlement to be reduced.
4. The EPS fallback selects the first associations when performance data is missing. EPS must be absent/pending until the two qualifying associations are known; it must never be guessed from the access ranking.
5. `addUntil` fills direct-stage vacancies from arbitrary domestic tables. UEFA titleholder vacancies are rebalanced through the annual access list and eligible club coefficients, not awarded to the next English or Israeli league finisher.

### Resolver invariant

Represent every place as a versioned entitlement with `association`, `competition`, `entryRound`, `route`, `source`, `priority` and `reallocationRule`. Then resolve in order:

1. ordinary league and cup outcomes;
2. UCL, UEL and UECL titleholder entitlements and lower-slot deductions;
3. EPS associations and recipient;
4. domestic cup/league cascades;
5. UEFA access-list rebalancing, licensing and eligibility.

The resulting list is qualification for the **next UEFA season**, not a label for participation in the domestic table's current season.
