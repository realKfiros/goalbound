# Goalbound Career Simulation

Goalbound models the sporting and employment decisions in one footballer's career, together with the club world that develops around that career.

## Language

**Club Offer**:
An actionable opportunity presented to the player, such as a permanent move, renewal, loan, promotion or decision to stay.
_Avoid_: Deal

**Contract Terms**:
The employment terms offered to the player: contract duration and weekly wage. Permanent moves and renewals create new contract terms; staying continues the existing contract.
_Avoid_: Transfer fee, transfer value

**Transfer Fee**:
The amount a buying club agrees to pay the player's current club for a contracted player. It is separate from the player's wages and contract duration.
_Avoid_: Wage, salary, contract value

**Former-club Return**:
A move back to a club for which the player genuinely appeared earlier in the same career. It may be a larger sporting step down than an ordinary market offer because the relationship itself is part of the move.
_Avoid_: Home-country move, familiar league

**Veteran Market**:
The credible destination set for an established older player, led by major leagues, prestige late-career leagues and genuine former clubs.
_Avoid_: Any lower-level club willing to offer a starting role
